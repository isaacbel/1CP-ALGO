A delivery company in Algiers aims to automate its services. 
Items to be delivered are defined by unique identifiers, 
entry dates, delivery wilayas (1 to 58), weights, and statuses.
The company uses motorcycles and vans, 
each with unique identifiers and maximum capacities, managed through text files.

To manage deliveries, linked lists (ItemL and VehicleL) and queues (MotoQ and VanQ) are utilized. 
ItemL stores delivery items, while VehicleL manages vehicles. 
MotoQ and VanQ are queues for motorcycles and vans, respectively, populated from text files.

Tasks include adding new items to ItemL, managing vehicles in VehicleL, 
and simulating delivery operations prioritizing items with earlier entry dates. 
Motorcycles have specific delivery restrictions, while vans handle deliveries outside Algiers.

Simulations also cover vehicle comebacks, return pickups, delivery cancellations,
 and end-of-day processes involving file updates and report generation on deliveries,
 returns, and new items.
